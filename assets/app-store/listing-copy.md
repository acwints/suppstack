## Description

SuppStack AI helps you find, compare, and organize supplements around the outcomes you care about.

Search products, brands, supplements, goals, and reference-only peptide information from one clean catalog. Build a stack before you buy, compare serving economics, and keep product actions simple: add, save, or continue to the merchant checkout path.

Shop by goal:
Sleep and recovery, body composition, training output, metabolic health, daily foundation, and focus support.

Compare products:
See brand names, product identity, price, cost per serving, official-store labels, and source context without digging through noisy pages.

Organize your stack:
Save products, add them to your routine, and keep supplement choices connected to your personal goals.

Reference-only peptides:
Peptide pages are separated from buyable products and include safety context. SuppStack does not sell peptides.

SuppStack AI is designed for wellness shopping and personal organization. It is not medical advice. Always talk with a qualified professional before starting, stopping, or changing a supplement routine, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or managing a medical condition.

## Review Notes

SuppStack AI is a Capacitor iOS shell for the production SuppStack web app. Native integrations include HealthKit plumbing, native in-app browser checkout handoff, OAuth deep-link return, splash/status bar configuration, and offline fallback.

The app sells physical goods through external merchant checkout paths, so In-App Purchase is not used for supplement purchases. Peptide pages are reference-only and do not include purchase links. Account deletion is available in-app from You/Profile settings.

No demo credentials are required. Reviewers can use Continue as guest to browse Shop, catalog search, brand pages, product pages, and peptide reference pages. Sign in is only required for saved personal stack state.
